# Make the driver and supervisor trip screen scrollable

## What changes
- Allow the opened trip window to scroll vertically and horizontally on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Remove the fixed overflow restriction that currently traps content inside the window.
- Keep the trip controls, map, student list, and student actions usable without clipping.

## Verification
- Open a trip at phone and desktop sizes and verify up/down and left/right scrolling.
- Confirm the map, student list, and end-trip controls remain accessible.
- Confirm the project remains error-free.
